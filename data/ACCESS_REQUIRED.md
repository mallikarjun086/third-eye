# RESTRICTED DATASET ACCESS PROCEDURES

The following datasets require formal academic EULAs and cannot be automatically scraped:

1. **CUFSF (CUHK FERET)**: Apply at [CUHK MMLab](http://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/archive/cufs/). Place zip in `data/cufsf/`.
2. **IIIT-D Forensic Sketch Database**: Submit EULA to IIIT-Delhi. Place in `data/iiitd/`.
